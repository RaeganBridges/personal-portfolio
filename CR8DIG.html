This project was about combining design, music culture, and interactive technology into a social media platform that allows people to share music and express what songs personally mean to them. I designed and coded the website almost entirely myself while using AI coding technology as a tool to speed up development, solve coding challenges, and help streamline certain systems. The platform allows users to annotate songs, create personal posts connected to music, comment on other users' content, search for artists or songs, and browse through different genres. I wanted the experience to feel both creative and community-driven, almost like a mix between music discovery and personal storytelling.

One of the most important parts of the project was building systems that made large amounts of music content easier to generate and organize. I used tools like lyric-fetching APIs to automatically generate song information and cover art more efficiently, which allowed me to focus more heavily on user interaction, visual design, and overall functionality. Throughout the process, I had to think carefully about interface design, navigation, responsiveness, and how users would emotionally connect with the platform. The project became not only a coding challenge, but also an exploration of how technology and AI can support creativity, self-expression, and online communities centered around music.
