This project focused on creating a minimal, design-led brand identity and packaging system for a salsa verde intended for everyday table use. Rather than emphasizing intensity or novelty, the goal was to position the product as a refined staple. It is something that feels equally at home as a functional condiment and as a considered object on the dining table. I explored how restraint in color, typography, and composition can communicate quality, freshness, and intentionality.

The packaging design was developed with simplicity and clarity in mind, using a clean layout and a limited color palette that reflects the natural qualities of salsa verde. The label avoids unnecessary visual noise, instead prioritizing legibility and balance to create a calm, elevated presence. I wanted to ensure that it felt practical for regular use while still maintaining a strong aesthetic appeal when placed on display.

Overall, the branding was guided by the idea that everyday products can also be beautiful. The final design positions salsa verde not just as a condiment, but as a table object that enhances both utility and atmosphere. It feels intentional enough to leave out, use often, and integrate seamlessly into a modern kitchen or dining environment.
