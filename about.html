About Me

I am a graphic designer currently studying at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where I am pursuing a BFA in Graphic Design with a minor in Entrepreneurship. My work is rooted in branding, visual storytelling, and creating design systems that shape how people experience everyday products, ideas, and communities.

Alongside my studies, I serve as the Social Media Leader for Delight Ministries, which has strongly influenced the way I approach design. In that role, I create content that communicates faith-based messaging in a clear, engaging, and visually consistent way. It has taught me how to design for real audiences with intention.

I also have a strong love for photography, which plays a key role in how I see and create visual work. Photography has trained my eye for composition, lighting, and storytelling in a more observational way, and I often bring that perspective into my design practice.

In my design work, I’m especially interested in branding and packaging, where I explore how typography, color, and form can communicate personality and purpose. I enjoy working at the intersection of creativity and strategy, using design not only to make things visually compelling, but also meaningful and functional. Through both my academic work and leadership experience, I continue to grow as a designer who values storytelling, intentionality, and impact.
