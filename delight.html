This campaign project was about creating a cohesive and realistic fundraising campaign for Delight at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville that would better connect with college students. Anna and I wanted to make the donation process feel clearer, more trustworthy, and more engaging through social media promotion, tabling events on campus, and physical collateral such as buttons, stickers, postcards, banners, and signage. I drew heavily from Delight’s existing branding, merchandise, and values to create a visual identity that felt consistent across all materials by using similar colors, typefaces, and aesthetics.

Throughout the project, collaboration and feedback played a major role in shaping our final direction. I was responsible for creating much of the physical collateral, organizing and formatting the presentation, helping photograph materials, and refining the overall campaign aesthetic alongside Anna. The process involved a lot of discussion, critique, and adaptation from both classmates and instructors. In the end, I think we created a campaign that felt achievable for a student-run organization while effectively targeting UTK students and strengthening Delight’s campus presence.
