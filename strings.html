This project was about honoring Dolly Parton through a decorative object that symbolized both her cultural impact and personal identity. I designed and handcrafted a functional clock inspired by Dolly's Appalachian roots, music career, femininity, and philanthropy. The overall shape of the clock was based on a banjo, referencing the traditional instruments and musical heritage connected to Appalachia and country music. I constructed the main body through woodworking, which gave the piece a handmade and grounded quality that reflected the authenticity often associated with Dolly herself. The clock hands were shaped like banjo picks, further reinforcing the connection to music and performance while also turning a small functional detail into part of the storytelling.

The decorative elements of the clock were deeply symbolic as well. I created layered butterflies using 3D printing and hand painting to represent transformation, individuality, freedom, and Dolly's larger-than-life personality. Butterflies are often associated with beauty and growth, which connected to both her visual image and the way she has continuously reinvented herself throughout her career while still remaining true to her roots. I also hand-painted roses onto the surface of the clock to symbolize femininity, compassion, love, and Southern beauty. In addition, the clock was hand bedazzled, reflecting Dolly Parton's signature sparkle and glam aesthetic, often associated with rhinestones and stage presence, symbolizing how she embraces boldness, visibility, and joy in her public image. The final piece became not only a functional clock, but also a tribute to Dolly Parton's influence on music, culture, philanthropy, and Appalachian identity.
