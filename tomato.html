This project was about exploring how the same subject can be interpreted and communicated through a variety of artistic mediums. I designed and created a short book centered around cherry tomatoes, using them as a consistent subject while recreating them through painting, sketching, collage, markers, typography, 3D construction, and AI-generated imagery. I wanted to show how different materials, textures, and creative processes can completely change the feeling, style, and meaning of the same object while still maintaining a cohesive concept throughout the book.

Throughout the process, I focused heavily on experimentation and visual consistency. Each medium presented different challenges and opportunities, forcing me to think about composition, color, texture, and form in new ways. Some methods felt more controlled and traditional, while others like AI and collage introduced unpredictability that became part of the creative process. Designing the book also required me to think carefully about pacing, layout, and how each page related to the next so the project felt unified despite the dramatic differences in style and technique. Overall, the project became both a study of artistic mediums and an exploration of how creativity can evolve when the same idea is approached from multiple perspectives.
